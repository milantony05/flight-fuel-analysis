      System methods and data status

      A layered evaluation workflow makes the model, decision rule and uncertainty behavior independently inspectable.

      1

      Fuel estimation

      The existing trajectory-and-aircraft feature model supplies interval fuel predictions.

      2

      Error ranking

      A separate risk score estimates which predictions are likely to be easier or harder.

      3

      Uncertainty calibration

      Five-fold isotonic calibration and scaled-ratio quantiles produce exploratory held-out diagnostics.

      4

      Coverage optimization

      A HiGHS mixed-integer solver minimizes predicted risk under overall and subgroup access constraints.

      Evaluation scope: the dashboard contains pre-scored historical benchmark intervals and works fully offline. The replay simulates sequential arrival for inspection; a live telemetry connector is not enabled. Reported uncertainty is cross-fitted on this benchmark without a formal coverage guarantee. The calibrated policy has indirect batch-level label dependence. Use earlier disjoint calibration data and an untouched later evaluation before deployment.
