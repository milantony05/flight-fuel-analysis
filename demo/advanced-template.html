Does it work for everyone?

An uncertainty range may contain the recorded fuel about 90% of the time overall, while missing more often for a particular aircraft. These are checks on historical data, not safety guarantees.

05 / Honest conclusion

Who this helps and what comes next.

What the project can honestly say
Problem, audience and contribution

Researchers studying aviation fuel and people building public flight datasets can use this audit to spot missing groups before drawing conclusions. It compares prediction error with which flights remain in the dataset. It is not a tool for airline fuel planning or official emissions accounting.

How the experiment works

Fuel model: April–August 2025. Error ranker: September 1–9. Main audit: September 19–27. Whole flights remain separated. Fuel-on-board differences are imperfect labels. Zero-position intervals use a training-only type-rate fallback. The exact selector minimizes accepted count first, then predicted risk under constraints; this need not minimize observed error.

Limitations and next test

Payload, weight and weather are missing. Routes recur. October was inspected during development. Calibrated selection has indirect batch-level label dependence. A later untouched batch with earlier disjoint calibration is required for confirmation. Row-bootstrap intervals do not account for flight clustering.
