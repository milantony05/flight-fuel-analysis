What this project can defend

Problem, audience and contribution

Aviation researchers and public flight-data curators need to know whether filtering estimates removes the aircraft and durations they intend to study. This project audits error, access and empirical reliability together. It does not plan operational fuel or certify emissions.

How the experiment works

Fuel model: April–August 2025. Error ranker: September 1–9. Main audit: September 19–27. Whole flights remain separated. Fuel-on-board differences are imperfect labels. Zero-position intervals use a training-only type-rate fallback. The exact selector minimizes accepted count first, then predicted risk under constraints; this need not minimize observed error.

Limitations and the next test

Payload, weight and weather are missing. Routes recur. October was inspected during development. The calibrated selector can have indirect batch-level label dependence through competing scores. A later untouched batch and earlier disjoint calibration are required for confirmation. Row-bootstrap intervals do not account for flight clustering.
