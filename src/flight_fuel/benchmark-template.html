What this demo can and cannot establish

Fuel is estimated retrospectively for completed intervals whose boundaries came from ACARS reports. April–August trained the fuel predictor; September 1–9 trained an error-ranking model; September 19–27 supplies this labeled batch. The policy and revised model were designed after examining earlier October results, so these comparisons are exploratory. The revised model disables the historical model’s row-random internal early-stopping split. It is a dataset audit, not a calibrated per-flight guarantee, live flight forecast, or verified emissions inventory.

The reference labels are differences between onboard fuel reports, and public ADS-B may be absent. Recorded fuel share is observable only when evaluating a labeled dataset; a new unlabeled batch cannot know its true fuel share. See the README for definitions, regeneration steps, and limitations.
